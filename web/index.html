Dig Duel

A two-player fossil hunt for the Seeed reTerminal Sticky. Two Stickies find each other over ESP‑NOW and play directly, with no Wi-Fi network or app.

Install

  1. Connect a Sticky to this computer with a USB-C data cable and switch it on.
  2. Click Install Dig Duel and choose the Sticky's serial port. It usually shows up as USB Single Serial or a CH343 device.
  3. Wait for the install to finish, then unplug it and repeat with the second Sticky.
Your browser can't flash devices. Use Chrome or Edge on a desktop computer. Open this page over HTTPS to flash.

Installing replaces the Sticky's current firmware, including Seeed's note app. You can put Seeed's firmware back later from Seeed's Sticky firmware page.

How to play

  1. Switch both Stickies on near each other. They pair automatically.
  2. Bury. Your four fossils (skull, femur, claw, tooth) start in random spots. Tap one to pick it up, then tap a square to move it. Tap it again or press ROTATE to turn it, or press SHUFFLE for a new random layout. Press I'M READY when you're done.
  3. Dig. On your turn, tap a square of the other player's site, then press DIG. Find a bone and you dig again.
  4. Radar. Twice per game you can press RADAR instead: it counts the bones in the 3×3 area around the square you picked, and uses up your turn.
  5. The first player to unearth all 12 bones wins. Each Sticky then reveals its map, and the other one checks it against the answers it was given during the game.

Press the AI button once to redraw the screen and clear e-paper ghosting. Hold it for 2 seconds to put the Sticky to sleep; press it again to wake up. A Sticky also falls asleep by itself after 15 minutes without play.

Source code and build instructions: github.com/mmeyer724/sticky-dig-duel. Flashing uses ESP Web Tools.
